Apply these settings

  1. Install and enable this App's bundled Home Assistant Executa.
  2. Open Anna Executa settings and enter HA_URL and HA_TOKEN.
  3. Select your access rules above, then click Copy policy JSON.
  4. Paste into the Executa's HA_POLICY_JSON credential and save.
  5. Use Read Executa policy to verify the effective settings.
