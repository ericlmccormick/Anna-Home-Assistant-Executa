Set up Home Assistant

  1. Check your Anna Agent. In Anna, open Agents and confirm the agent you use for chat is online.
  2. Open the connector’s credentials. Click your profile in Anna, then go to Advanced → Executa → Learned → Home Assistant Connector → Credentials.
  3. Connect your server. In the credential form, enter your HTTPS Home Assistant base URL in HA_URL and your long-lived access token in HA_TOKEN. Click Save, then return here and select Test connection.

Apply permissions

  1. Choose your access levels below and select Copy policy JSON. Sensor reads are enabled by default. To list lights or check their status, use Other entity states. Lights controls actions such as turning them on or off.
  2. Return to the same credential form, paste the JSON into Permissions policy (JSON) (HA_POLICY_JSON), and click Save. Then select Read Executa policy here to confirm. Save draft keeps your edits in Control Center until you apply them in the credential form.
  3. If you choose Verify, configure an approval method under Set up verification below. Review and approve pending actions in Identity verification.

Enter your Home Assistant access token only in Anna’s Executa credential form.
